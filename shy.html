Feeling Shy

Feeling shy means you may need extra time before joining in or speaking. You can be quiet and still be brave, kind, and important.

Try saying

"I want to join, but I need a small first step."

Small steps count

Watch first

It is okay to take a moment to understand a new place or group.

Start small

A wave, smile, or one-word greeting can be a courageous beginning.

Bring support

A trusted person can help you practise or walk in with you.

Try this now

  1. Practise one sentence. Try "Can I play too?" or "Hi, my name is..."
  2. Use a brave breath. Breathe in slowly, breathe out, then take one small step.
  3. Celebrate effort. Joining for one minute or asking one question is progress.

For grown-ups

Avoid labelling a child as "the shy one." Give preparation, warm encouragement, and time without pressure.
