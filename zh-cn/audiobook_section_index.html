When I Can't Do It Yet

When I Can't Do It Yet

Sometimes kids try their very best and still cannot get it right. Shoelaces stay untied. Letters come out wobbly. Blocks tumble down.

This story gently reminds children that not being able to do something now does not mean they never will.

Children practise naming frustration, breathing slowly, asking for help, and replacing "I can't" with "I can't yet."

Little steps turn into big progress.
