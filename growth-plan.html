Parent scripts

What to say when...

Short phrases for moments when a child needs connection before correction.

Your child says, "I'm not good enough."

"It feels really hard when you compare yourself. Let's look at one thing you can do now that you could not do before."

Build self-esteem

Your child refuses to try.

"Your brain is saying this feels too big. We can make it smaller. What is the first tiny step?"

Try small steps

Your child feels left out.

"That hurts. You wanted to belong. I am here with you while we think about what might help next."

Understand lonely feelings

Your child is angry.

"Anger is telling us something matters. Let's help your body calm first, then we can talk about what happened."

Explore anger

It is bedtime.

"Today is done. Your body can rest now. Tomorrow we can try again with a fresh start."
