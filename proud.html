Feeling Proud

Pride is the warm feeling that can grow when you try, learn, help, or show courage. You do not need to be perfect to be proud of your progress.

Try saying

"I am proud that I kept trying and learned something."

Healthy pride can come from

Effort

Working on something even when it takes time or practice.

Courage

Being yourself or trying something that felt a little scary.

Kindness

Helping someone, apologising, or making a caring choice.

Try this now

  1. Finish the sentence. "I am proud of myself for..."
  2. Notice the journey. Draw or describe what you practised and what helped.
  3. Share the spotlight. Celebrate your progress and notice someone else's effort too.

For grown-ups

Offer specific praise such as, "You stayed patient and tried another idea," so children connect pride to actions and growth.
