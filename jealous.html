Feeling Jealous

Jealousy can show up when someone gets attention, a turn, or something you wanted. The feeling is okay; what matters is choosing kind actions.

Try saying

"I wish I had a turn too. Can we make a plan?"

Notice what is underneath

A wish

You may want a turn, an object, or a chance to succeed too.

A worry

You may wonder whether you are still loved, included, or important.

A comparison

Comparing can hide the good things that are growing in you.

Try this now

  1. Name the wish. Say what you hoped for without blaming someone else.
  2. Choose one kind action. Congratulate, take turns, or ask for help making a fair plan.
  3. Remember your own good. Name one strength, effort, or happy moment that belongs to you.

For grown-ups

Validate the wish before guiding behaviour: "It is hard to wait. You may not grab, but I can help you ask for a turn."
