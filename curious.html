Feeling Curious

Curiosity is the feeling that makes us wonder, notice, and ask questions. It helps children learn about themselves, other people, and the world.

Try saying

"I wonder why that happens. How could we find out safely?"

Ways curiosity grows

Observe

Look closely, listen, and notice what changes or stays the same.

Ask

Good questions begin with why, how, what, or what if.

Discover safely

Ask an adult before exploring anything private, risky, or unfamiliar.

Try this now

  1. Collect three wonders. Write or draw three questions about today.
  2. Choose one investigation. Read, observe, ask an adult, or test a safe idea.
  3. Share what changed. Explain one thing you know now that you did not know before.

For grown-ups

You do not need every answer. Try, "That is a thoughtful question. Let us see how we can find out together."
