Printable emotional learning tools

Worksheets for calm, clear practice.

Use these printable activities during quiet moments, classroom routines, or gentle follow-up conversations. They are designed to help children name feelings, slow down, reflect, and practise emotional skills with a trusted adult.

How to use them well

  • Choose one worksheet that matches the child’s current need.
  • Read the prompts together before asking a child to complete it alone.
  • Use the activity to start a conversation, not to test or correct.

Gentle use

Before you print

Worksheets are most helpful when they support connection and reflection rather than pressure.

For children

Let children skip questions, draw instead of write, or come back later. The goal is expression, not perfect answers.

For parents

Use one prompt as a conversation starter. If a child becomes overwhelmed, pause and return to connection first.

For teachers

Use worksheets during calm routines, reflection time, or small-group support. Avoid using them as punishment.
