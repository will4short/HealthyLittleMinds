Feeling Bored

Boredom can be your brain saying, "I need something interesting, meaningful, or restful to do." It is not a bad feeling; it can be the beginning of an idea.

Try saying

"I feel bored. Can I choose something to make, move, or help with?"

What boredom might be telling you

I need a spark

Your mind may want a challenge, a question, or something creative.

I need movement

A stretch, dance, or outside play can help energy find a direction.

I need connection

Sometimes boredom is a wish to play, talk, or help someone.

Try this now

  1. Pick three choices. Draw, build, read, help, or move; then select one.
  2. Set a ten-minute mission. Make a paper invention, tidy a small area, or find five blue things.
  3. Ask a wonder question. "How does that work?" can turn boredom into curiosity.

For grown-ups

Offer a small menu rather than filling every quiet minute. Unstructured time can help children practise choosing and imagining.
