Values

Discover what matters and let actions—big or small—line up with those values.

Overview

Values point to the kind of person we want to be—kind, curious, brave, fair. They’re not goals you “finish,” but directions you can follow every day. This page offers simple ideas to help kids and teens notice values and turn them into small, doable actions.

Try It Now

  • Top-3 values: circle three words that fit you today (e.g., kindness, learning, friendship).
  • Value → Action: pick one value and one tiny action for this week that shows it.
  • Look back: name one moment you lived a value recently—what did you do and why?

Talk Starters

  • “Which moments make you proud of how you acted?”
  • “If this value could talk, what would it ask you to do next?”
  • “What helps you choose your value when it’s hard in the moment?”

Let Values Guide Decisions

Values are directions, not tests. A person can choose kindness, courage, fairness, or learning even after a difficult moment.

Discover

Think about people you admire and what their actions reveal about what matters to you.

Decide

When choices are hard, ask: “Which option moves me closer to the person I want to be?”

Practise

Choose one small weekly action, such as welcoming someone, telling the truth, or trying again.
