Feeling Love

Love can feel warm, safe, and connected. It grows through caring words and actions while always respecting each person's body, space, and choices.

Try saying

"I care about you. Would you like a hug, help, or some space?"

Ways love can show

Care

Listening, comforting, helping, and remembering what matters to someone.

Belonging

Knowing you matter, even when you make mistakes or have big feelings.

Respect

Kind love accepts "no," asks permission, and gives safe space.

Try this now

  1. Share appreciation. Tell someone one kind thing you notice about them.
  2. Offer a helpful act. Tidy together, make a note, or listen without interrupting.
  3. Practise consent. Ask before a hug or touch, and respect the answer kindly.

For grown-ups

Help children learn that love includes affection, repair after conflict, and body-safety boundaries that are always respected.
