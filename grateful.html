Feeling Grateful

Gratitude is noticing something good, kind, or helpful. It does not mean pretending hard feelings are gone; we can feel thankful and still have a difficult day.

Try saying

"Something that helped me today was..."

Things we can notice

People

A person who listened, helped, played, or made you feel cared for.

Moments

A laugh, a meal, a sunny walk, a rest, or something you learned.

Yourself

Your own effort, bravery, patience, or kindness today.

Try this now

  1. Name three small goods. Draw or share three things you noticed today.
  2. Thank someone clearly. Explain what they did and why it mattered.
  3. Pass kindness on. Choose one thoughtful action for another person.

For grown-ups

Model gratitude without forcing it. Acknowledge difficult moments first, then gently invite children to notice support or small bright spots.
