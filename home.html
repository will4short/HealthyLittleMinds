📚 Emotional Literacy: Books That Help With Feelings

Read and grow

Swipe, scroll, or let the shelf glide through stories that help children name and understand feelings.

Swipe the story shelf

🐒 Sometimes Family Finds You

"Sometimes Family Finds You" is a quiet, heartfelt story about belonging, resilience, and the many ways connection can begin. Through Bella’s gentle narration and Punch’s journey, children discover that feeling unseen does not mean they are unworthy—and that sometimes, family finds us in unexpected ways.

📘 Read Sometimes Family Finds You

🌊 The Day Goshen Learned to Guide the River

"The Day Goshen Learned to Guide the River" is a calm, reflective story about helping children manage big emotions through safe replacement behaviors. When Goshen observes a beaver guiding a river instead of stopping it, he learns a gentle way to help a friend—and himself—when feelings feel overwhelming.

📘 Read The Day Goshen Learned to Guide the River

👑 When My Feelings Get Too Big

"When My Feelings Get Too Big" is a gentle story that helps children understand big emotions and feel safe again. Through Princess’s experience, kids learn that feelings are not bad—they are messages. They practice pausing, breathing, and naming what they feel.

📘 Read When My Feelings Get Too Big

🌟 The Day Destiny Wanted To Quit

"The Day Destiny Wanted To Quit" is a heartfelt story about finding courage when giving up feels easier. It helps children see how small choices and support can help them keep going, even when life feels really hard.

📘 Read The Day Destiny Wanted To Quit

😨 When I Feel Scared

"When I Feel Scared" is a comforting story that helps children understand fear and find gentle ways to feel safe, supported, and brave again.

📘 Read When I Feel Scared

🌱 Ella and the Promise She Keeps Trying

"Ella and the Promise She Keeps Trying" follows Ella as she learns that effort matters more than being perfect. Through practice, mistakes, and courage, she discovers that trying again is part of growing stronger.

📘 Read Ella and the Promise She Keeps Trying

🌍 I Will Wait Till I Get Older

"I Will Wait Till I Get Older" follows Goshen, a 12-year-old boy who has lived in four different countries. It gently explores belonging, patience, and hope—showing that growth takes time.

📘 Read I Will Wait Till I Get Older

⚖️ The World Isn’t Just Black and White

"The World Isn’t Just Black and White" helps children practice flexible thinking, pause before reacting, and understand that feelings and situations are not always one way or the other.

📘 Read Grey Thinking

💖 Little Acts, Big Feelings

"Little Acts, Big Feelings" celebrates kindness, empathy, and the emotions that grow when children care for others and see the impact of small, loving actions.

📘 Read Little Acts, Big Feelings

🌧️ How to Calm the Storm Inside

"How to Calm the Storm Inside" helps children recognize overwhelming emotions and learn calming tools like breathing, drawing, and asking for help.

📘 Read How to Calm the Storm Inside

Why This Matters

Teaching emotional literacy helps kids understand their feelings, connect with others, and grow into kind, confident humans. Our books and tools are designed to make this learning gentle, joyful, and practical.

Name feelings Children find words for what is happening inside.
Practise coping Small calming tools become easier to use when needed.
Connect with care Families can talk, listen, and ask for support sooner.
