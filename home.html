🐒 Sometimes Family Finds You

Recommended for ages 13+ "Sometimes Family Finds You" is a quiet, heartfelt story about belonging, resilience, and the many ways connection can begin. Through Bella’s gentle narration and Punch’s journey, children discover that feeling unseen does not mean they are unworthy—and that sometimes, family finds us in unexpected ways.

📘 Read Sometimes Family Finds You

🌊 The Day Goshen Learned to Guide the River

Recommended for ages 13+ "The Day Goshen Learned to Guide the River" is a calm, reflective story about helping children manage big emotions through safe replacement behaviors. When Goshen observes a beaver guiding a river instead of stopping it, he learns a gentle way to help a friend—and himself—when feelings feel overwhelming.

📘 Read The Day Goshen Learned to Guide the River

👑 When My Feelings Get Too Big

Recommended for ages 9+ "When My Feelings Get Too Big" is a gentle story that helps children understand big emotions and feel safe again. Through Princess’s experience, kids learn that feelings are not bad—they are messages. They practice pausing, breathing, and naming what they feel.

📘 Read When My Feelings Get Too Big

🌟 The Day Destiny Wanted To Quit

Recommended for ages 12+ "The Day Destiny Wanted To Quit" is a heartfelt story about finding courage when giving up feels easier. It helps children see how small choices and support can help them keep going, even when life feels really hard.

📘 Read The Day Destiny Wanted To Quit

😨 When I Feel Scared

Recommended for ages 12+ "When I Feel Scared" is a comforting story that helps children understand fear and find gentle ways to feel safe, supported, and brave again.

📘 Read When I Feel Scared

🌱 Ella and the Promise She Keeps Trying

Recommended for ages 9+ "Ella and the Promise She Keeps Trying" follows Ella as she learns that effort matters more than being perfect. Through practice, mistakes, and courage, she discovers that trying again is part of growing stronger.

📘 Read Ella and the Promise She Keeps Trying

🌍 I Will Wait Till I Get Older

Recommended for ages 13+ "I Will Wait Till I Get Older" follows Goshen, a 13-year-old boy who has lived in four different countries. It gently explores belonging, patience, and hope—showing that growth takes time.

📘 Read I Will Wait Till I Get Older

⚖️ The World Isn’t Just Black and White

Recommended for ages 13+ "The World Isn’t Just Black and White" helps children practice flexible thinking, pause before reacting, and understand that feelings and situations are not always one way or the other.

📘 Read Grey Thinking

💖 Little Acts, Big Feelings

Recommended for ages 6+ "Little Acts, Big Feelings" celebrates kindness, empathy, and the emotions that grow when children care for others and see the impact of small, loving actions.

📘 Read Little Acts, Big Feelings

🌧️ How to Calm the Storm Inside

Recommended for ages 6+ "How to Calm the Storm Inside" helps children recognize overwhelming emotions and learn calming tools like breathing, drawing, and asking for help.

📘 Read How to Calm the Storm Inside

Emotional literacy in everyday life

Why This Matters

Emotional learning is not one big lesson. It grows through small moments: naming what is happening, practising a helpful response, and staying connected with trusted adults.

Start with language children can use.

When children can name a feeling, adults can respond with more patience and children are less likely to feel alone with what is happening inside.

Explore feelings

Practise before the hard moment.

Simple routines like breathing, drawing, pausing, and asking for help become easier when children practise them during calm moments.

Open worksheets

Connection makes learning safer.

Healthy Little Minds resources are designed to help adults guide, listen, and co-regulate rather than shame, rush, or correct too quickly.

Meet the Creator

Healthy Little Minds® is created by Godswill U. Ambrose to make emotional learning practical, gentle, and accessible for children, parents, and teachers.

The work is guided by a simple responsibility: explain psychology clearly, avoid overstatement, and turn useful ideas into resources people can actually use in homes, classrooms, and everyday conversations.

01 Educational focus

Resources are designed to teach emotional skills through simple, repeatable practice.

02 Evidence-informed approach

Psychology is presented carefully, with respect for uncertainty and real-world context.

03 Practical support

Stories, worksheets, and guides are shaped for real families, classrooms, and quiet moments.
