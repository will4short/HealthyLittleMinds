🌟 Scene 6 – The Story You Choose

Bella learned something big today:
every choice she made led to a different path.

Being scared, staying quiet, speaking up — all of them mattered.
And even when things felt tricky, she kept trying.

🌈 Your choices matter too.
They can help you grow stronger, braver, and kinder every day.

🧠 Parent Tip: Every child’s choices — whether bold or quiet — shape who they are becoming. Encourage reflection, not perfection. Help them feel proud of their growth.
