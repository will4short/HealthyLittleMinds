😐 Scene 2 – Bella Pretends

Bella takes a deep breath and smiles.
But inside, she still feels nervous.
She doesn’t tell anyone and just pretends everything is fine.

“No one needs to know… I can just act like I’m not scared.”
👪 Parent Tip: Pretending to be okay can be a way kids hide feelings. Help your child feel safe sharing what’s really inside.
