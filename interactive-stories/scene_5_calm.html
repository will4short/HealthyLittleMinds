🌈 Scene 5 – I Feel Calm Now

Bella walks out of the classroom with a light heart.

She smiles gently to herself — she’s glad she spoke up.

The scary feeling isn’t gone forever, but now it doesn’t feel so big.

“It’s okay to be nervous… I’m still proud I said something.”
🧠 Tip for Parents: When children share, they don’t need perfect words — they need presence. Thank them for opening up and remind them it’s brave to speak, even in small ways.
