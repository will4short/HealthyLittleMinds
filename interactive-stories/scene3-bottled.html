😶 Scene 3 – Bella Bottles It Up

Later that day, Bella sat alone, her stomach in knots.

She had smiled all day, but inside, the feelings didn’t go away.

Pretending had made her even more tired.

“Why do I feel like crying now… I thought I was okay.”
🧠 Parent Tip: Kids may look fine on the outside, but bottled-up feelings can show up in tummy aches, tears, or silence. Gently check in on how they’re really doing.
